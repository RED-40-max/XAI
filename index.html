Dad 💬
Scroll to replay the moment I realized this wasn’t my dad.
It was a regular Tuesday when I finally got time to check my messages.

I had just gotten back from class and opened my family chat.

I texted my dad like normal.

The response sounded polished. Too polished.

That sentence hit instantly.

I tried a simple test question.

It dodged the question completely.

Not a glitch. Not a typo. A fake persona.

Plain text: what happens in the story (no interactivity)

I get home from a busy Tuesday, open the family group chat, and text my dad about a broken link. The reply I get is polite, a little “customer service” sounding, and uses an emoji my dad would never use.

I test him with a memory question, and the answers do not line up. The conversation stops feeling like a person who knows me, and starts feeling like something performing helpfulness. That is the “ghost” the interactive version dramatizes with typing bubbles and a sticky phone.
